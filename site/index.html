    How Addis Bites works

    From your address to your door in four steps

    We built this around how food actually moves in Addis — the courier goes to the counter, pays the kitchen from our float, and brings your food straight to you.

    1

    Tell us where you are

    Pick your sub-city and we show only the kitchens that can actually reach your street — never a restaurant that will cancel on you.

    2

    Choose your food

    Browse the dishes, build your basket and see the delivery fee up front. What you see is what you pay.

    3

    A courier is dispatched

    Our courier rides to the kitchen, places your order at the counter, and Addis Bites pays the restaurant for you — so your food is never held up over payment.

    4

    Eat, then pay how you like

    Pay cash to the courier on arrival, or settle by Telebirr or CBE. Each order carries a code you can quote if anything needs sorting.
